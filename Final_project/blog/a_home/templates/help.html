Account & Settings

How do I edit my profile?

Updating your profile takes just a few steps:

  1. Log in to your account
  2. Click your avatar in the top-right corner
  3. Select "Edit Profile" from the dropdown menu
  4. Update the information you want to change
  5. Click Confirm

What if I forgot my password?

If you forgot your password, reset it with these steps:

  1. On the login page, click "Forgot Password"
  2. Enter the email address you used to register
  3. Follow the instructions sent to your email to reset your password

If you don't receive the reset email, check your spam folder or contact support.

How do I change my email address?

Changing your contact email requires identity verification:

  1. After logging in, go to Settings
  2. Select "Change Email"
  3. Verify your identity
  4. Enter your new email and complete verification

User Guide

How do I get started quickly?

New user quick start:

  1. Complete registration and verify your email
  2. Fill in your profile
  3. Explore the home page content
  4. Check the tutorials to learn about features

How does search work?

Our search supports multiple filtering options:

  • Keyword search: enter what you're looking for directly
  • Advanced search: use filters for precise results
  • Tag search: click a tag to view related content

What's different between mobile and desktop?

Our platform offers a consistent core experience across devices, with a few differences:

Feature Mobile Desktop
Layout Vertical layout, suited for one-handed use Multi-column layout with richer information display
Shortcuts Swipe gestures Keyboard shortcuts

We recommend trying the platform on different devices for the best experience.
